# Downstream coordination

Replies and hand-off material **addressed to the repositories that consume our
exported partitions**. This is the mirror of the consumers' own `docs/upstream/`
folder: they stage requests to us there, and nothing in either direction is
applied by editing the other repository.

Who consumes what, and what each reader enforces, is
[`../reference/DOWNSTREAM_CONSUMERS.md`](../reference/DOWNSTREAM_CONSUMERS.md).
This folder holds the correspondence itself.

| File | Topic |
|---|---|
| [`issue-4-collapsed-faces-reply.md`](issue-4-collapsed-faces-reply.md) | Reply to **surface-partition#4** — collapsed (zero-area) sub-faces and coincident vertices in the Rep-3 mesh |
| [`degenerate_faces.yaml`](degenerate_faces.yaml) | The measurement behind that reply, regenerated by `scripts/check_degenerate_faces.py --emit` |

**Every number in a reply must come from a committed script**, the same rule as
`docs/math/` and `docs/experiments/` — a reply is quoted back at us later, so it
is held to the reporting standard, not to the standard of a chat message.
